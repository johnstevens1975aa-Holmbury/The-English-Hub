Reading for Inference

Find meaning beneath the surface of words.

Core explanation

A strong answer defines the key concept, applies the skill, uses an example, and explains why the reasoning is accurate.

Glossary with definitions and examples

inference
A meaning worked out from clues.
Example: “His hands trembled” may imply fear.
evidence
A quotation used to support a point.
Example: The phrase “cold silence” supports the idea of tension.
analysis
Explaining how language creates meaning.
Example: The verb “stumbled” suggests uncertainty.

Shakespearean Conflict

Character, theme, dramatic irony and context.

Core explanation

A strong answer defines the key concept, applies the skill, uses an example, and explains why the reasoning is accurate.

Glossary with definitions and examples

soliloquy
A speech spoken alone revealing thoughts.
Example: Macbeth’s soliloquies reveal ambition and fear.
dramatic irony
When the audience knows more than a character.
Example: The audience knows danger is coming before the character does.
theme
A recurring big idea.
Example: Power is a theme in many tragedies.

More than one possible meaning.
